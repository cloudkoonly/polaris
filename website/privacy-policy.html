1. Who we are

Polaris Wing Technology Limited is an AI-first technology company that designs and builds software across AI, SaaS and mobile applications. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), we are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us at contact@polariswing.com.

This policy applies to our websites (including polariswing.com), our mobile and desktop applications distributed through app stores such as Google Play and the Apple App Store, and any related services that link to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide to us. This includes your name and email address when you create an account or contact us; the content of support messages; information you submit through forms or surveys; and any content you upload or type into our products, including prompts and files submitted to AI-powered features.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Services we may collect device and app information (device model, operating system and app version, language and region settings, and a device or installation identifier), IP address, general location inferred from IP address at city or country level, in-app events and usage data, and diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance metrics.

Information from third parties. If you install or purchase an app through an app store, the store may share limited information with us, such as the fact of a purchase, subscription status, or aggregate, non-identifying reports about installs and crashes. Payment card details are handled by the app store and are not received by us.

Sensitive information. We do not ask you for special categories of personal data (for example health, biometric or political data) and we ask that you do not submit such information to our products, including in prompts or uploaded files.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • provide, operate and maintain the Services, including account creation and authentication;
  • process transactions and manage subscriptions and entitlements;
  • respond to support requests, questions and complaints;
  • personalize and improve features, including understanding which features are used and where they fail;
  • monitor performance, debug crashes, prevent abuse, fraud and security incidents;
  • communicate with you about service changes, security notices and, where permitted, product news;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

AI features. Content you submit to AI-powered features is processed to generate the response you requested. We do not use your content to train generalized AI models, and we do not sell your content. Where we rely on third-party model providers, we configure those services not to retain or train on your content where such controls are available. Do not submit information to AI features that you are not permitted to share, and review outputs before relying on them.

4. Legal bases for processing (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process personal information on the following legal bases: performance of a contract (providing the Services you requested); legitimate interests (securing and improving the Services, preventing abuse); consent (where we ask for it, such as optional marketing communications); and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share personal information only as described below:

  • Service providers. Vendors that help us operate the Services, such as cloud hosting, content delivery, analytics, crash reporting, email delivery and customer support tooling. They may process personal information only on our instructions and under written agreements.
  • AI model providers. Where a feature depends on a third-party model, the relevant content is transmitted to that provider to return a result, under the provider's terms and our configuration settings.
  • App stores and platforms. As necessary for distribution, purchases and subscription management.
  • Legal and safety. Where required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or Polaris Wing.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to this policy and appropriate confidentiality protections.
  • With your direction. When you ask us to share information, or consent to a specific sharing.

6. International transfers

We operate globally and may process personal information in countries other than the one where you live. Where personal information from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with additional technical and organizational measures where required.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account data: while your account is active, and for up to 90 days after deletion, so that deletion can be completed and disputes resolved.
  • Support communications: up to 24 months after the request is closed.
  • Analytics and product usage data: typically up to 14 months, in aggregated or de-identified form where possible.
  • Server and security logs: typically 30 to 90 days.
  • Billing and tax records: as long as required by applicable law.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls and least-privilege permissions, logging and monitoring, and vendor review. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information; to obtain a portable copy; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes requiring a limitation right.

To exercise any right, email contact@polariswing.com. We will verify your request using the information we hold about you and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows it. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Account and data deletion

If a Polaris Wing application offers an account, you can delete your account from within the app (typically under Account or Settings). You can also request deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time by emailing contact@polariswing.com with the subject "Data deletion request" from the address associated with your account. We will confirm the request and complete deletion within 30 days, except for information we are required or permitted to retain (for example, transaction records for tax purposes or data needed to prevent fraud), which we keep only for as long as the law requires.

11. Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law, including in parts of the EEA and the UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Cookies and similar technologies

Our websites may use strictly necessary cookies to operate the site and, where you consent, analytics cookies that help us understand aggregate usage. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the site works.
